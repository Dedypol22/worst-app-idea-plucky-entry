> MirrorShare is a marketplace where you rent out your own reflection to strangers who want to look confident before important meetings, starting at $4.99 an hour.

Why it lands: it names a specific app and its useless job in one sentence, pitched with deadpan founder confidence — a marketplace, a target user, and an hourly price for something that is already yours, free, and attached to you. The absurd logic (renting out your own face by the hour) and the vivid picture of a stranger borrowing your reflection before a job interview make the uselessness funny without ever winking at the joke.
