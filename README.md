> QueuePal is an app that hires someone to stand in line for you at the DMV, then live-streams their boredom to your phone so you still feel like you waited, ticket number and all.

Why it lands: it names a specific app and its useless job in one sentence, pitched with deadpan founder confidence — a service, a concrete setting, and a target user — and then the app carefully undoes its own solution: you pay to skip the DMV queue, then watch a stranger's boredom live so you still experience the queue, ticket number and all. The vivid picture of someone filming their own boredom on a plastic DMV chair while you watch from your couch makes the uselessness funny without ever winking at the joke.
